Welcome to The Allied Society's Website!

This is the official website for Padblocker's Allies, a group that fights discrimination and censorship throughout websites such as Scratch and CodeTorch. Over time, this also became the official website for the micronation known as the Oceanside Republic. Now, this website is home to Wykentrian information (I'm working on it), such as the link to the Discord server (click the flag) and the Wykentrian Anonymous Chat.

All new members of The Allies must thoroughly read and understand the Constitution to be an Ally.

Note: Discord Server

If you want to join the Discord server, you will have to wait until a moderator approves your application. This is simply to ensure security for all fellow Wykentrians, and will not harm you for trying to join.
