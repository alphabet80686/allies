Welcome to The Allied Society's Website!

This is the website I (A29854) originally made for a group I am a part of -- the Allies. Over time, this also became the official website for the micronation known as the Oceanside Republic. Now, this website is home to Wykentrian information (I'm working on it), such as the link to the Discord server (click the flag) and the Wykentrian Registry.

General Note

ALL members of The Allies must thoroughly read and understand the Constitution to be an Ally.

Note: Discord Server

If you want to join the Discord server, you will have to wait until a moderator approves your application. This is simply to ensure security for all fellow Wykentrians and will not harm you for trying to join.

Current News

  • Woffical has parted from Wykentria due to drama across platforms taking a mental toll on him. We understand his decision completely, and if you are reading this, Woffical, we wish you peace in the future and an escape from the drama that compelled you to leave.

    He was a good citizen, a good person, and most of all a good friend. It is sad to see some of our fellow Wykentrians leave from drama, but we can resist! As hard as it is, try not to let people get to your head -- a lot of times, they only want to start trouble. If you suffer from drama, or are just feeling down in general, feel free to talk to your friends about it if you wish. We only want to help!

    View Lxrd Wykea's project on CodeTorch for a message that summarizes our feelings for Woffical. We will never forget you!
